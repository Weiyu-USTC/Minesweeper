EFFICIENCY, WITHOUT THE MYSTERY

Fewer actions. More field.

Speed measures time. Efficiency measures how economically you solve a particular board.

THE MINESWEEPER ONLINE DEFINITION
Efficiency = 3BV ÷ actions × 100%

3BV counts one action for each connected zero opening, plus one for each safe numbered cell outside all openings. It is the perfect no-flag benchmark for a known board.

A 50-3BV board solved in 40 actions scores 125%. Flags and chords can beat the no-flag benchmark.

THE CHORD DECISION

Will this flag pay for itself?

Compare the independent reveals you save with the new flags and one chord you spend. Several cells in the same opening count as just one reveal.

Save 2 actions

Local comparison, assuming the number is already revealed and these reveals are otherwise needed. Reusing a flag can improve later chords.

01

Find the opening

Avoid individually revealing number cells that a safe zero opening will clear. Prefer a proven safe opening when you can identify one.

02

Chord selectively

One new flag plus one chord should replace at least three independent reveals. An existing flag makes nearby chords cheaper.

03

Look one move ahead

A neutral chord can expose a better number. Compare overlapping groups and shared flags before committing.

04

Keep goals separate

A higher score can cost thinking time or win probability. Track efficiency, time, and wins together on the same board family.
